Available adjacent stress cases in the Biopython corpus
-------------------------------------------------------
- `tricky.fastq`
- zero-length records
- truncated title/sequence/plus/quality cases
- too-short and too-long quality strings
- invalid quality-control characters
- CRLF/DOS files
- Illumina/Solexa/Sanger full-range encoding examples
- long-read fixtures around 8-9 KiB

Those are intentionally not all mirrored yet; this branch first keeps one normal and one structurally awkward valid specimen.
