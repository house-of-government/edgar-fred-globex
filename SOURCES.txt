GEOJSON SOURCES
===============

Specification
-------------
RFC 7946, The GeoJSON Format:
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7946

GDAL GeoJSON driver documentation:
https://gdal.org/en/stable/drivers/vector/geojson.html

USGS earthquake GeoJSON feed documentation:
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php

Samples
-------
sample-earthquakes.geojson
  Selected real USGS earthquake records from the archived `iTowns/iTowns2-sample-data` GeoJSON dataset.
  Upstream commit: c015c354e19d21ff56ae5b3149f127122a9917f8
  Path: geojson/Earthquakes_3857.geojson
  The upstream dataset reprojects USGS point coordinates to EPSG:3857 and retains the older GeoJSON `crs` member, making it useful as a legacy/interoperability specimen.

sample-polygon.geojson
  Source: OSGeo/gdal
  Commit: d6fd56f52d904c605fb1cc493bfd40fc976e7c0b
  Path: doc/data/polygon.geojson
  Blob SHA: 76329d16d203320cb64fba378abea40483963361
  Simple polygon ring with explicit closure and CRS84 legacy `crs` metadata.

Larger available stress input
-----------------------------
`muxlab/map-effects-100/data/earthquakes.geojson` is an archived USGS magnitude-4.5+ monthly feed containing hundreds of features. Link it when a large FeatureCollection stress case is useful rather than making the first branch fixture unnecessarily large.
