FASTQ
=====

Purpose
-------
FASTQ is a useful streaming/parser follower because it pairs sequence text with an equally long quality payload whose punctuation can look structural. It tests more state than FASTA without requiring XML/JSON machinery.

Common record shape
-------------------
A simple record is:
1. `@` title line
2. sequence
3. `+` separator line (optionally repeating an identifier)
4. quality characters with total length equal to sequence length

Real files may wrap sequence and quality across multiple physical lines, so a robust parser cannot always assume exactly four physical lines per record.

Important distinctions
----------------------
- `@` starts a record only in the correct parser state; it can occur in quality text.
- `+` is structural only after the sequence portion.
- total quality-character count must match total sequence-character count.
- FASTQ quality encodings historically include Sanger/Phred+33, older Solexa and Illumina variants; parser structure and quality-score interpretation are separate concerns.
- sequence motif search should search sequence bytes, not quality bytes.
- wrapped records require counting rather than blindly taking one sequence and one quality line.
- zero-length, truncated, overlong/short quality, and invalid-quality cases are valuable later rejection fixtures.

Samples
-------
`sample-small.fastq`: three ordinary short records.
`sample-complex.fastq`: three long Sanger records whose quality payloads are wrapped across multiple lines and contain punctuation including `@` and `+`.

First parser/search checks
--------------------------
- exact motif search only in sequence state;
- same motif text in quality state must be ignored;
- wrapped quality must accumulate to the sequence length;
- next `@` is a record marker only after the previous record is complete.
