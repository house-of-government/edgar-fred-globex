HDF4 sources
============

The HDF Group
-------------
https://support.hdfgroup.org/hdf4/
https://support.hdfgroup.org/documentation/
https://support.hdfgroup.org/releases/hdf4/documentation/HDF4_Users_Guide.pdf

The HDF4 User's Guide is the useful low-level anchor for descriptor semantics. It describes tag/reference identity, 32-bit offset/length fields, and linked data-descriptor blocks.

Format identification
---------------------
https://www.loc.gov/preservation/digital/formats/fdd/fdd000228.shtml

The Library of Congress format description records the HDF4 magic bytes `0E 03 13 01` and distinguishes HDF4 from HDF5.

Earth-observation relevance
---------------------------
https://www.hdfgroup.org/solutions/hdf4/
https://wiki.earthdata.nasa.gov/spaces/DAS/pages/170801036/Library-Tools

NASA/HDF-EOS material is important because HDF4 remains a live archival format for Earth-science products even though HDF4 itself is legacy/deprecated for new designs.
