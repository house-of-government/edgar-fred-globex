Format/reference documentation
------------------------------
NCBI BLAST FASTA sequence input documentation:
https://blast.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/doc/blast-topics/

NCBI nucleotide/IUPAC sequence conventions are useful when expanding beyond the A/C/G/T canary; link current NCBI documentation rather than mirroring manuals here.
