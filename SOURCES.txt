Notes
-----
NOAA's GOES-R beginner guide documents Level 1b/2+ products as netCDF-4 and calls out dimensions plus attributes such as `_FillValue`, `valid_range`, `scale_factor`, `add_offset`, and `units`. This makes GOES a good follow-on after a structural netCDF reader: the parser has to distinguish representation values from physical values and preserve missing/invalid states.
