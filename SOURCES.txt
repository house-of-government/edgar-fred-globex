CCSDS Space Packet sources
==========================

CCSDS 133.0-B-2 — Space Packet Protocol
---------------------------------------
https://ccsds.org/Pubs/133x0b2e2.pdf

The primary normative anchor. Relevant sections:
- 4.1.2: Space Packet = 6-octet primary header + 1..65536-octet data field;
- 4.1.3: primary-header layout;
- 4.1.3.3: packet type, secondary-header flag and 11-bit APID;
- 4.1.3.4: sequence control;
- 4.1.3.5: 16-bit packet data length, encoded as data-field octets minus one;
- 4.1.4: packet data field and mission-managed secondary-header contents.

GOES-R concrete follower
------------------------
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ncdc%3AC01570

NOAA documents GOES-R ABI Level 0 as CCSDS instrument/spacecraft packets stored inside a netCDF-4 byte array and identifies APID as the field used to support interpretation of packet type/content.

Scope rule
----------
This branch owns generic Space Packet framing only. GOES APID tables, instrument secondary-header formats and science payload decoding belong in the GOES/product layer rather than being baked into a supposedly generic CCSDS parser.
