MODIS / HDF-EOS2 sources
========================

MODIS Level-1B product summary
------------------------------
https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/pdf/MOD_02.pdf

This is a compact official description of the calibrated/geolocated radiance product and its multiple spatial resolutions/bands.

MODIS/HDF4 clarification and packed-data example
------------------------------------------------
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/s3fs-public/imported/MODIS_C6_Fire_User_Guide_A.pdf

The MODIS fire guide includes a useful explicit warning that HDF4 and HDF5 are incompatible formats and discusses HDF packed-data scaling. Product-specific scaling rules must still be taken from the product's own attributes/specification rather than generalized blindly.

HDF-EOS conversion context
--------------------------
https://wiki.earthdata.nasa.gov/spaces/DAS/pages/170800220/HEG%2BHDF-EOS%2Bto%2BGeoTIFF%2BConversion%2BTool

HEG's existence is itself useful evidence for the interface problem: NASA supplies tooling to subset/reproject/reformat HDF-EOS objects into more ordinary GIS forms such as GeoTIFF.
