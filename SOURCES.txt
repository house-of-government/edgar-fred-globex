Why use archived copies
-----------------------
These are frozen parser specimens. The real source family is SEC EDGAR; live retrieval should not be part of compiler correctness. Preserve exact sample bytes here and use SEC documentation/filings as provenance and differential oracles.

Documentation policy
--------------------
Link official SEC documentation rather than mirroring entire manuals/specifications here unless redistribution terms are clear. The point of this branch is a readable inventory plus representative parser bytes, not a duplicate SEC documentation site.
