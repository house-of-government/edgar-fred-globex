Sentinel-2 SAFE sources
=======================

SentiWiki: SAFE format
----------------------
https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/safe-format

SentiWiki: Sentinel-2 products
------------------------------
https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s2-products

The Sentinel-2 product documentation states that Level-1C and Level-2A user products are made available in Sentinel-SAFE format, with JPEG 2000 image data, XML product metadata, quality indicators, auxiliary material and XSD schemas.

Useful schema/document trail
----------------------------
The SentiWiki product page links the current Sentinel-2 Product Specification Documents and XSDs. Prefer keeping the product/schema version with every fixture rather than copying a schema into the parser and forgetting where it came from.

Fixture policy
--------------
For future fixtures, preserve:
- exact product identifier;
- processing level;
- product/schema version where available;
- source/download URL or catalog identity;
- a small manifest/XML specimen separately from large JP2 image assets;
- checksums for any binary fixture retained outside this staging repository.

First cross-format acceptance target
------------------------------------
Given one real Sentinel-2 product, enumerate its product/granule metadata and prove that one named band asset exists, parses as JP2, and agrees with the metadata on the dimensions/resolution facts chosen for the test.
