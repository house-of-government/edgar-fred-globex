FBI CDE JSON SOURCES
====================

Official source
---------------
FBI Crime Data Explorer:
https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/

The CDE provides interactive exploration and downloadable reported-crime/UCR/NIBRS data. Endpoint families evolve, so branch samples are frozen response specimens; live CDE is the authoritative current source.

Frozen response examples mirrored here
--------------------------------------
Both samples are from the documented/test example corpus in `apsamuel/statpack`, whose examples preserve CDE response shapes used by its FBI adapter.
