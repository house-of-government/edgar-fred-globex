HDF-EOS sources
===============

NASA Earthdata
--------------
https://wiki.earthdata.nasa.gov/spaces/DAS/pages/170801036/Library-Tools
https://wiki.earthdata.nasa.gov/spaces/DAS/pages/170801032/SDP%2BToolkit%2Band%2BHDF-EOS%2BEOSDIS%2BCore%2BSystem%2BProject
https://wiki.earthdata.nasa.gov/spaces/DAS/pages/202211788/Toolkit%2BDownloads

These pages describe HDF-EOS as standard HDF plus ECS conventions/metadata and identify Grid, Swath and Point as the principal geolocation structures.

EOSDIS / HDF-EOS5 standard
---------------------------
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/s3fs-public/imported/ESDS-RFC-008-v1.1.pdf

The RFC describes the HDF-EOS5 data model and its mapping to HDF5. It is also useful for keeping HDF-EOS semantic structures separate from the HDF5 storage layer.

EOSDIS handbook
---------------
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/s3fs-public/imported/EOSDIS_Handbook_1.5.pdf

The handbook gives an accessible description of Grid, Swath and Point and the processing contexts in which each appears.

MODIS follower
--------------
https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/pdf/MOD_02.pdf

MODIS Level 1B calibrated/geolocated radiance products are a concrete HDF-EOS example and make a good domain-level parser target after the generic HDF-EOS layer works.
